5) REGLES METIER MISES EN PLACE
- Validation pays via module country (CountryRepository/CountryContextService).
- Validation account holder et delegation via identity (AccountHolderService/AccessControlService).
- Validation montants offre (min/max/interet) et montant demande vs offre.
- Workflow statut applications:
  DRAFT -> SUBMITTED -> UNDER_REVIEW -> APPROVED -> DISBURSED -> REPAID
  et transitions vers REJECTED selon etape.
- Scope partner backoffice:
  acces borne au partner_id via PartnerBackofficeAccessService.
- RBAC partner:
  PARTNER_ADMIN / PARTNER_ANALYST / PARTNER_VIEWER controle selon endpoint/action.
- Ecritures partner ledger:
  APPLICATION_SUBMITTED, APPLICATION_APPROVED, DISBURSEMENT, REPAYMENT.
- Integration ledger existant:
  Pour DISBURSED/REPAID: OperationService + AccountingPostingService.
- Reporting:
  mise a jour snapshot quotidien + rapport quotidien portfolio.
